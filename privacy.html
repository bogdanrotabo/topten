Privacy

Last updated 25 August 2026.

TopTen.one has no accounts and no logins. We collect as little as a public leaderboard can work with.

What we store

For each listing: the profile link you submit, the handle derived from it, an optional 80-character tagline, the running total paid towards it, and the time of the last payment. All of this is public by design — it is the board.

For each payment: the Stripe session identifier, the amount and the currency, linked to the listing it paid for. This is not shown publicly.

What we do not store

No card numbers, no billing addresses, no email addresses, no passwords, no accounts. Payments are processed entirely by Stripe, which acts as its own controller for the payment data you give it. We receive back only the amount and a session identifier.

We do not ask who you are when you submit a listing, and we cannot tell you who paid towards one.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count page views and see which boards get traffic. It sets its own cookies and processes an abbreviated IP address. See Google's privacy policy. Browser Do Not Track and content blockers are respected — the site works fine without it.

Avatars

Profile pictures on the boards are fetched by your browser from unavatar.io using the public handle. That request comes from your browser, so unavatar.io sees your IP address. We do not upload or host any images of people.

Local storage

We keep one value in your browser's local storage: the id of the listing you last paid for, so the thank-you page can find it after you come back from Stripe. Clearing your browser data removes it. It is never sent anywhere except to look up a public board row.

Being listed by someone else

Anyone can submit any public profile link, including yours. If you want a listing about you removed, email hello@topten.one with the link and we will take it down. We do not require proof of identity for a removal request, and no refund is owed to whoever paid for it.

Retention and your rights

Listings stop being displayed 30 days after their last payment. Listing and payment records are kept for accounting purposes. Under Swiss data protection law and the GDPR you may ask for access, correction or deletion of data concerning you — write to hello@topten.one.

Where things run

The site is served by GitHub Pages through Cloudflare. The database and the payment webhook run on Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt). Payments run on Stripe. TopTen.one is operated from Switzerland.
