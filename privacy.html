PRIVACY

Last updated 6 September 2026.

TopTen.one has no accounts and no logins. We collect as little as a public ranking can work with.

What we store

For each listing: the name you submit, an optional link, the running total paid towards it, and the time of the last payment. All of this is public by design — it is the ranking.

For each payment: the Stripe session identifier, the amount and the currency, linked to the listing it paid for. This is not shown publicly.

For each visit: a random identifier kept in your browser, the address you landed on, the site that sent you, your browser's language and a two-letter country. And, if you press them, which buttons: a ranking opened, an amount chosen, a payment begun, a share pressed. No text you type is ever recorded, and none of it is shown publicly.

What we do not store

No card numbers, no billing addresses, no email addresses, no passwords, no accounts, no IP addresses. Payments are processed entirely by Stripe, which acts as its own controller for the payment data you give it. We receive back only the amount and a session identifier.

We do not ask who you are when you submit a listing, and we cannot tell you who paid towards one.

Where the country comes from

The site sits behind Cloudflare, which tells the page which country the request came from. That two-letter code is what we keep. Your address is not stored.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count page views. It sets its own cookies and processes an abbreviated IP address. See Google's privacy policy. Browser Do Not Track and content blockers are respected — the site works fine without it.

Removal

Write to hello@topten.one and we will take a listing down. Payment records are kept, because they are an accounting record of money that changed hands.
