Privacy

Last updated 6 September 2026.

TopTen.one has no accounts and no logins. It collects as little as one paid position on one page can be run with.

What we store

For each reign and each attempt: the amount, the currency, the name Stripe collected at the till, and the time. For a reign, also the message and link its holder wrote. All of that is public — it is the page.

Not shown publicly: the Stripe session identifier for the payment, and the key that lets its payer edit their card. Neither can be read with the public key this site hands to your browser; they are withheld by the database itself, not by the page.

What we do not store

No card numbers, no billing addresses, no email addresses, no passwords, no accounts. Payments are processed entirely by Stripe, which acts as its own controller for the payment data you give it. We receive back the amount, the currency, a session identifier and whatever name you gave Stripe.

If you would rather not have a name on the page, do not give Stripe one — the card simply reads "Anonymous", and you can write any name you like on it afterwards.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count page views. It sets its own cookies and processes an abbreviated IP address. See Google's privacy policy. Content blockers are respected — the site works fine without it.

Local storage

Your browser keeps one thing: the key to any card you have paid for, so you can edit it again later. It is sent only to this site, and only to save your own card. Clearing your browser data removes it and there is no way to get it back.

Being written about by somebody else

A king writes their own card and could write anything on it. If one names or describes you and should not, email hello@topten.one and it comes down. We do not require proof of identity for a removal request, and no refund is owed to whoever paid for it.

Retention and your rights

Reigns and attempts are kept indefinitely: the list of former kings is the history of the page, and payment records are kept for accounting. Under Swiss data protection law and the GDPR you may ask for access, correction or deletion of data concerning you — write to hello@topten.one.

Where things run

The site is served by GitHub Pages through Cloudflare. The database and the two functions behind it run on Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt). Payments run on Stripe. TopTen.one is operated from Switzerland.
