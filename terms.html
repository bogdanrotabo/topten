A reign is a single payment. Payments are not cumulative: a second payment is judged on its own against whoever is king at the time it settles, and does not add to an earlier one.

While nobody holds the page, any payment of one unit of the charged currency or more takes it.

Payments are judged in the order they settle, not the order they were started. If two payments arrive at once, the one recorded first is compared first, and the second is then compared against the result.
