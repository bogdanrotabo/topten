Payments are final

There are no refunds, under any circumstances. Payment buys a position on a public board at the moment of payment and nothing more. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card details.

The minimum payment is US$2. Amounts are charged in US dollars.

Listings expire after 30 days

A listing stays on its board for 30 days from its most recent payment. After that it becomes inactive and stops appearing publicly. Its historical total is kept, and any new payment reactivates it with that full total intact. We do not send reminders before a listing expires.
