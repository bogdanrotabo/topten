TopTen.one

About

Everything online is ranked by something nobody will explain: an algorithm, a follower count, a paid partnership that does not admit to being one. TopTen.one does the opposite. Thirty-four boards, ten places on each, and the only thing that moves you up is money. Social profiles and the creators on them, crypto, memecoins, giveaways, football, F1, games, artists, podcasts, cities, restaurants, startups, pets. The number to beat is printed at the top of every board, and again on the row directly above you.

How it works

Pick a board, say what you are listing — a profile, a coin, a club, a city, a dog — pick an amount, pay. Your listing appears on that board at whatever position the money buys, immediately. There is no review, no waiting and no account to create.

Payments are cumulative. A listing's rank is the total ever paid towards it, so $20 today and $30 next week is a $50 listing. Anyone can add money to any listing — including someone else's, if they want to push a friend up the board.

Positions 11 and below sit in the waiting list under each board, with the exact amount it would take to break into the Top 10.

Listings expire

Thirty days after its last payment a listing drops off the board. Its total is kept, so a single new payment brings it back exactly where the money says it belongs. Nobody owns a position forever.

Why it is honest

Paid placement is everywhere; it is just usually disguised as a recommendation. Here there is nothing to disguise. Rank is a receipt. If someone is at #1, you know precisely why, and you know precisely what it would cost to take it from them.

The rules

Illegal, hateful, adult, spam and impersonation listings get hidden, without a refund. If you are the subject of a listing you did not ask for, email hello@topten.one and it comes down.

Read the terms before paying — particularly the part where payments are final.

Who runs it

TopTen.one is a small independent project built and operated from Switzerland. It is not affiliated with X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Telegram, Snapchat, Twitch, LinkedIn or Threads. Say hello: hello@topten.one
