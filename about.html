About

One page. One king.

Everything online is ranked by something nobody will explain: an algorithm, a follower count, a paid partnership that does not admit to being one. This site does the opposite, and it does it with one position instead of a leaderboard.

There is a single seat. Whoever has paid the most for it is on it, their name and their message are the page, and the figure it cost them is printed underneath. That is the entire product.

How it works

Pay whatever you like. If it is more than the king paid, the page is yours immediately — you fill in a name, a message and a link, and the previous king moves into the list of former kings. If it is the same or less, the king stays where they are and your payment is recorded, publicly, as an attempt on them.

There is no top-up. A reign is one payment. Paying twice does not add up to one larger figure; the second payment is judged against the king on its own, which may well be yourself.

There is no expiry. A reign lasts until somebody outpays it. It can last an hour or a year.

Why the attempts are shown

Because they are the price of the seat, honestly stated. A king who cost $40 and has survived nine attempts is worth something different from a king who cost $40 and nobody has challenged, and hiding the second number would make the first one a boast rather than a fact.

Why it is honest

Paid placement is everywhere; it is usually disguised as a recommendation. Here there is nothing to disguise. The position is a receipt. If somebody is on the page you know exactly why, and you know to the cent what it would cost to take it from them.

The rules

Illegal, hateful, adult, spam and impersonating cards are removed, without a refund. If a king has written something about you that should not be there, email hello@topten.one and it comes down.

Read the terms before paying — particularly the part where payments are final.
